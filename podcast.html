Shifting Tides

An audio essay exploring how social values in Bangladesh have changed over the last decade, created as part of my MBA coursework.

Changes in Social Values in Bangladesh: The Last Decade

A reflection on how economic development, technology, Western media, education, entrepreneurship, family life, health awareness, and globalization have influenced changing social values in Bangladesh. The episode also considers the resistance and concerns surrounding these changes.

🎙️ Produced with Adobe Podcast AI enhancement

Why this topic?

Bangladesh has experienced significant economic, technological, and social change over the past decade. This audio essay examines how those changes have influenced everyday attitudes, family life, education, work, culture, and aspirations, while reflecting on both the opportunities and concerns they have created.
